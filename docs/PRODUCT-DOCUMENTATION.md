# PRODUCT-DOCUMENTATION.md — Fiesta Agency

## Product Purpose

The Fiesta Agency website solves three business problems:

1. **Brand presentation** — Showcases Fiesta's work, style, and capabilities in a way that matches the quality of their events
2. **Lead generation** — Converts visitors into booking inquiries through the contact form
3. **Content management** — Allows the Fiesta team to update website content, manage events, and track bookings without developer intervention

---

## Target Users

### Prospective Clients
People looking for an event agency for their wedding, corporate event, or private celebration. They browse the website to evaluate Fiesta's style, see past work, and decide whether to make contact.

### Existing Clients
People who have worked with Fiesta and want to explore additional services or share the website with others.

### Website Visitors
General visitors who arrive through search, social media, or referrals. They may be researching event agencies, exploring Fiesta's portfolio, or looking for event inspiration.

### Administrators / Content Managers
Fiesta team members who log into the admin panel to:
- Update page content and sections
- Add/edit events and portfolio projects
- Manage testimonials and FAQs
- Handle booking inquiries
- Upload and organize media
- Configure site settings

---

## Customer Journey

```
Discover Fiesta (search, social, referral)
      ↓
Visit Homepage (hero, services, events, testimonials)
      ↓
Explore Services (detailed service descriptions)
      ↓
Explore Events (filterable gallery, event details)
      ↓
Explore Portfolio (project stories, galleries)
      ↓
Learn About the Agency (story, mission, values, team)
      ↓
Understand the Process (How We Work — five phases)
      ↓
Contact / Plan Your Event (form submission)
      ↓
Booking Stored (Supabase) + Email Sent (Web3Forms)
      ↓
Admin Reviews Booking (BookingsAdmin)
      ↓
Follow-up Communication (outside system)
```

---

## Business Value

### For the Business
- **Professional online presence** that matches the quality of Fiesta's events
- **Lead capture** through the booking inquiry form
- **Content control** — the team can update the website without developer involvement
- **Portfolio showcase** — visual proof of capability that builds credibility
- **SEO visibility** — proper meta tags, sitemap, and structured content for search engines

### For Visitors
- **Clear understanding** of what Fiesta does and how they work
- **Visual proof** through event photography and project stories
- **Easy contact** through the inquiry form
- **Mobile-friendly** experience on any device

### For Administrators
- **Dashboard** with overview of events, bookings, and stats
- **Visual page builder** for composing pages from sections
- **Collection management** for events, portfolio, testimonials, FAQs
- **Media library** for organizing and referencing images
- **Booking tracking** with status management
